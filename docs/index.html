Northeastern academic calendar, as an iCalendar feed

Northeastern publishes its academic calendar only as a PDF. This rebuilds it weekly as an .ics feed, filtered to Boston-campus undergraduate events.

Subscribe

In Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL, paste that, and subscribe. It always serves the newest academic year (currently 2026-2027), so you never re-subscribe.

Subscribed feeds are read-only and Google refreshes them on its own schedule — often up to 24 hours. If you want the events editable, or want them immediately, download a file below and import it instead.
