Northeastern academic calendar, as iCalendar feeds

Northeastern publishes its academic calendar only as a PDF. This rebuilds it weekly as .ics feeds, split by audience and category so you can subscribe to exactly the slice you want.

Pick a feed

The whole university-wide calendar is the root. Each audience is a branch; the big ones split further by category. Subscribe at whatever depth suits you — one link per calendar you want.

In Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL, paste one, subscribe. Subscribe to several and colour them separately.

Every feed tracks the newest academic year automatically (currently 2026-2027), so you never re-subscribe.

Subscribed feeds are read-only and Google refreshes them on its own schedule — often up to 24 hours. For editable events, download a file and use Import instead.
